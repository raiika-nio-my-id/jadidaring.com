JADE ROOM   /   MAHJONG, TOGETHER.Gold tile = new draw · Enter to discard · Arrow keys to moveA good game, together.

A good hand starts here.

Two to four players take turns drawing and discarding tiles. The host can start with the people at the table, or fill empty seats with bots. Empty seats are skipped when bots are off. Make four melds and a pair, or seven distinct pairs, to declare Mahjong.

  • Chow: three consecutive tiles in the same suit. You may claim only from the player immediately before you.
  • Pung: three identical tiles. Claim a discard from any player.
  • Kong: four identical tiles. Claim a discard or declare a concealed Kong, then draw a replacement.
  • Mahjong: claim a winning discard or win on your own draw. Win claims outrank Pung/Kong, which outrank Chow. Equal claims go to the closest player clockwise.

Claims have a 30-second window. Your turn has a 120-second limit; inactive players automatically discard. The round ends when someone wins or the wall runs out.

Matchmaking: join a public waiting table first. Otherwise spectate a playing table and queue for the next round. Queued spectators take bot or vacant seats when the host starts the next round. Private rooms are only found by their invitation code.

The latest unclaimed discard has a gold outline. Your newest drawn tile is highlighted in gold and automatically sorted into your hand. Its highlight clears after your discard.

Use the arrows below the center tiles to browse earlier discards. Latest returns to the newest page. Opponents’ exposed melds appear as tile faces beside their concealed hand.

This is a casual classic variant: no flowers, minimum fan, scoring, added Kongs, robbing Kongs, or multi-winner claims. Each new round rotates the dealer to the next occupied seat.
