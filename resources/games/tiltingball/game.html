A gentle guide

Go with the roll.

Tilt: Tap “Play with tilt”, allow sensor access if prompted, then calibrate with the phone facing up. Tip its edges to steer. Recalibrate anytime under Controls.

Touch: Press the board and drag in the direction you want to roll. Releasing slows the ball. Desktop: use arrow keys or WASD.

The goal: Find the green finish hole. Dark holes send you back to the start. There’s no time limit and you can retry as often as you like.

Your unlocked levels and best times are saved on this browser.
